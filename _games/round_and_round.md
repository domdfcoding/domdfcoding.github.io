In Round and Round miners have harnessed a device to put the mine in a loop. The promise of infinite gemstones is quickly shattered when the entire earth becomes stuck in the loop. You need to gain access to the control room and stop the loop before it's too late.

Collect gemstones to sell, and use the proceeds to buy keys and upgrades to help you find the keycard, access the control room, and shutdown the loop generator.But make sure you're back in the safe zone before the loop resets the map - it's the only part of the planet that isn't stuck in the loop!

[Game Maker's Toolkit Game Jam 2025](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2025) Entry. The theme was "Loop". As well as the time loop, the map itself loops around top to bottom.
